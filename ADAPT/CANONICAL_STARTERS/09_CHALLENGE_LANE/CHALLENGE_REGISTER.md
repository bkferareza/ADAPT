## Purpose

This register tracks all challenge reviews initiated within this ADAPT instance. Every challenge must be routed through the Director and recorded here upon initiation.

## Challenge Type Values

- Assumption Challenge
- Failure Mode
- Evidence Quality
- Scope Question
- Risk Identification

## Status Values

- OPEN — challenge submitted, not yet under active review
- IN_REVIEW — challenge is being assessed by the Challenge Lane
- RESOLVED — challenge addressed with documented evidence or corrective action
- DISMISSED — challenge found to be unsubstantiated; Director confirmed dismissal
- DEFERRED — challenge acknowledged but deferred to a future milestone
