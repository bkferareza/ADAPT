## Purpose

This file contains summary reports for completed onboarding operations. A report is created for each onboarding that reaches COMPLETE or REJECTED status. Partial or failed onboardings must also be recorded to support audit.

## Blueprint Depth Values

- FULL — all required agents defined with all 8 required sections; quality check passed
- PARTIAL — one or more agents are missing sections; onboarding paused pending correction
- DEPTH_FAILURE — DEFAULT_AGENT_BLUEPRINT.md did not meet the minimum standard; onboarding halted and ROLE_AGENT_BLUEPRINT_DEPTH_FAILURE reported to Director
