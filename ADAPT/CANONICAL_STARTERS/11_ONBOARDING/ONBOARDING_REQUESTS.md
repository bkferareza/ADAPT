## Purpose

This log records all onboarding requests received by the Director. Every request must be logged before the onboarding sequence begins, regardless of outcome.

## Status Values

- PENDING — request received; onboarding sequence not yet started
- IN_PROGRESS — onboarding sequence is actively running
- COMPLETE — onboarding succeeded; workcell registered and first handoff emitted
- REJECTED — onboarding could not proceed (pre-onboarding requirement not met, blueprint depth failure, or Director decision); reason recorded in Notes
