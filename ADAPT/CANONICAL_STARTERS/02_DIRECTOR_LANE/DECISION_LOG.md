## Status Values

- ACTIVE: Decision is in effect

- REVERSED: Decision was reversed; reason recorded in Notes

## Notes

Decisions must be traceable to the source truth version under which they were made. A superseded decision should not be deleted — mark it SUPERSEDED and reference the replacement DEC-ID. DEC-IDs follow format DEC-NNN.
