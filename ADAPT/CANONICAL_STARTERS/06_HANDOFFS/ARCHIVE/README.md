# Archived Handoffs

STATUS: DRAFT
PROJECT: {{PROJECT_NAME}}

## Purpose

Contains handoffs from completed or superseded work items, archived by the Janitor for historical reference.

## Archive Policy

The Janitor moves handoffs only from CONSUMED/ to ARCHIVE/ after their milestone
closes, per HANDOFF_GUARDRAIL.md. It never discharges handoffs out of ACTIVE/.
The following inform archive-candidate review without bypassing milestone closure:
- The milestone they belong to is closed
- The work item they represent is accepted or deferred
- The Cleanup Reporter determines they are no longer needed for active tracing

## Notes

Archived handoffs are read-only. Retrieval is possible for audit or challenge review purposes.
