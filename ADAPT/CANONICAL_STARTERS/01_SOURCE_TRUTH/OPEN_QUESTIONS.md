## Status Values

- OPEN: Question raised; no resolution yet
- IN_REVIEW: Under discussion with relevant authority
- RESOLVED: Answer accepted and recorded
- DEFERRED: Not needed for current milestone; reviewed later
- SUPERSEDED: Made irrelevant by a subsequent decision

## Priority Values

HIGH (blocks current work) / MEDIUM (affects upcoming work) / LOW (informational)

## Notes

Open questions that block execution must also be recorded as GAP-T10 (Decision Gap) or GAP-T11 (Context Gap) in GAP_REGISTER.md. OQ-IDs follow format OQ-NNN.
